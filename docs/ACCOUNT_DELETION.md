# Account Deletion — Robux Box

You can permanently delete your Robux Box account and all associated data
directly from the app — no email or web form needed.

## How to delete your account

1. Open **Robux Box** and sign in.
2. Go to **Profile → Settings**.
3. Scroll to **Delete account** and tap it.
4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.
5. If you signed in a while ago, the app may ask you to sign in again first —
   this is a standard security check before a permanent, destructive action.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

## What gets deleted

- Your profile (display name, photo, email/phone on file, Roblox username).
- Your coin wallet and balance.
- Your activity history: ads watched, offers completed, redemptions,
  referrals, achievements and badges.
- Your support tickets and messages.
- Your device and advertising identifiers on file.

## What may be retained

Some records may be kept for a limited time where required for fraud
prevention, abuse investigation, financial record-keeping, or to comply with
the law — for example, a record of a completed Robux/gift-card redemption, or
logs used to investigate account abuse. This mirrors section 5 of our
[Privacy Policy](./privacy.html). We do not use retained records for any
other purpose.

## Deleting without opening the app

If you no longer have the app installed and cannot sign in, contact us at the
support address in the [Privacy Policy](./privacy.html) from the email
associated with your account and we will delete your account and data
manually.
