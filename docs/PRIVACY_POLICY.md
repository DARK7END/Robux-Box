**[COMPANY NAME]** ("we", "us") operates the Robux Box mobile application (the
"App"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. Robux Box is
**not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Roblox Corporation**.

## 1. Information we collect
- **Account data:** email address, phone number, display name and profile photo
  (via Firebase Authentication / Google Sign-In), and your Roblox username if you
  provide it for redemption.
- **Location:** approximate and, with your permission, precise device location,
  used to determine your rewards **tier**. Coordinates are rounded before
  transmission and precise location is not stored.
- **Device & usage data:** a per-install random device identifier, device model/
  OS, app version, advertising identifier, and — with your consent — app-usage
  signals used to verify offerwall tasks.
- **Activity data:** coins earned/spent, ads watched, offers completed,
  redemptions, referrals, and in-app interactions.

We do **not** collect precise contact lists, SMS content, or biometric data.

## 2. How we use information
- Operate the App: credit rewards, process redemptions, run leaderboards.
- Determine your rewards tier from location.
- Prevent fraud and abuse (device/velocity checks, App Check, integrity signals).
- Send notifications and reminders you have enabled.
- Analytics and crash reporting to improve the App.

## 4. Data sharing
We share data only with: service providers (Google Firebase/AdMob, the offerwall
provider, reward-fulfilment partners) to operate the App; and authorities where
legally required. We do **not** sell your personal data.

## 5. Data retention & deletion
We retain account data while your account is active. You can delete your account
at any time in **Settings → Delete account**, which removes your profile, wallet
and activity from our systems (some records may be retained where required for
fraud prevention or legal compliance).

## 6. Children
The App is intended for users **[13/16/18]+**. We do not knowingly collect data
from children below the applicable age. If you believe a child has provided data,
contact us for removal.

## 7. Security
Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Sensitive local values are stored in the
device Keystore/Keychain. Access to balances is server-controlled.
